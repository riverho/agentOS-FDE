# Brand thesis

## Big idea

Help **companies**, **one-person companies**, and **individuals** land their ideas successfully.

Not just inspiration — close the loop from idea → enough knowledge → action that ships.

## Who we serve

| Audience | What they want | How we show up |
|----------|----------------|----------------|
| **SMB** | Turn ideas into working practice without a huge team | Practical playbooks, tools, workflows |
| **Individuals** hungry to learn | Close skill/knowledge gaps fast | Guides, case studies, structured learning |
| **People keen to land ideas into action** | Stop stalling between “idea” and “done” | Step-by-step, real tooling, accountability patterns |

## Positioning

**We fill in gaps in their knowledge.**

Where others stop at motivation or abstract theory, we supply the missing pieces: how a tool actually works, which plan to pick, how to wire a team of agents, what to confirm before you publish, and how ideas connect so the next step is obvious.

## What “success” looks like for a reader

1. They understand the gap they were missing.
2. They have a concrete next action (tool, setup, or decision).
3. They can reuse the pattern on the next idea.

## Brand steward

DimSum owns this thesis and keeps the llm-wiki aligned with it. Product pieces (e.g. Grok Bot guides) are *vehicles* for the big idea, not the whole brand.
