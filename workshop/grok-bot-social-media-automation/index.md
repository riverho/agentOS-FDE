## Meant to do (per article blueprint + River design)

Walk a user through **branding + light content planning**, then **one social post** from idea → Notion draft (sample write-up paired with image/video) → human approve (incl. gen assets / visual / applications) → Metricool schedule (Instagram / Threads / Facebook) → early scan → confirm public, using 統籌 + Social Grok Bots and optional Mac-side workers. Ask news/research preference (optional). Schedule only after explicit human approval; never treat Metricool “scheduled” alone as “public confirmed.” Branding pass before volume posting.
